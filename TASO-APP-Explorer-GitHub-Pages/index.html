Exploring Access and Participation Plans

Access and Participation Plans set out how higher education providers intend to improve equality of opportunity. TASO's APP Explorer helps sector colleagues review published plan content, including interventions, equality risks and targets.

These results are evidence-informed pilot findings. They are intended to support exploration and discussion, not provider ranking, formal judgement or automated replacement of human review.

Methods and Limitations

The page is designed to make the pilot useful while keeping its limits visible.

How to read the data

  • The datasets cover the APPs included in the approved publication only.
  • Information is extracted into structured fields and checked before publication.
  • Confidence labels reflect review confidence in the classification, not provider quality.
  • Records may combine direct evidence with interpretation; use the quotation and source reference to check context.

What not to infer

  • Do not compare providers as a league table or performance score.
  • Do not assume absence from the dataset means absence from the original APP.
  • Do not treat extracted findings as causal claims about impact.
  • Use human review before formal analysis, commissioning or policy decisions.
