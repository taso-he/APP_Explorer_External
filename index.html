Exploring Access and
Participation Plans

Every English higher education provider registered with the Office for Students publishes an Access and Participation Plan, setting out its targets and the activities it plans to deliver. Find, compare, map and download them through the APP Explorer. It is here to support collaboration and shared learning between providers.

Use with care

Records are copied from published plans, in providers' own words, and reflect each plan when it was published. Check the original plan before relying on any record.

Using the data well

Before you use the data

  • Plans can change after they are published. Formal variations to APPs may not be included, depending on when they were published. Providers may also have adjusted their interventions in response to new insight.
  • Some interventions are still being checked. Those marked "Awaiting review" have details that TASO has yet to confirm against the plan.
  • Check the original plan. Targets and activities are recorded in providers' own words, and every record links to the plan it came from. We have grouped them by lifecycle stage and student group so they can be filtered.
  • Not finding a provider doesn't mean it isn't working on a gap. Its plan may describe the work differently, or its interventions may not have been added yet.
  • This is not a league table. The number of targets or activities reflects how a plan is written, not how much a provider does or how well. Please don't use the data to rank or judge providers.

How the data was made

  • Targets come from the standard target tables in each current plan. Every target was checked against its plan, and corrected where needed.
  • Interventions come from the activity tables in each plan's intervention strategies. They were extracted using AI, then checked against the plan by a second AI model and by people at TASO.
  • Provider information (region, mission group, size and location) comes from OfS data. Regional access partnerships are as proposed, and may change.
